Longer transcripts are split into parts, summarized one after another and shown as pages. Roughly 3–4 characters ≈ 1 token. Lower this if you get "rate limit" or "too large" errors (check your provider's per-minute input token limit); raise it for fewer, longer parts. Default 80,000.

Overrides the default summary format. When a transcript is split into parts, each request is told which part it is (e.g. 2/3) and gets the earlier parts' summaries; whole-video sections such as conclusions are asked for only in the final part.
